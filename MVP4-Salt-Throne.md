# MVP4 — Salt Throne (balanced + shippable, playable 75–90 min)

**Goal:** no new mechanics. Lock balance, add finale scoring, variants, and publishable assets.
**Requires:** MVP3 = base rules (`How-to-Play.md`).

## Lock list (tuning only)

- Finale Kingsmoot bonuses (add after Season 5): +3 most Favor, +2 most successful raids, +1 per 5 Hoard banked. Crown = most Legend total.
- Balance knobs (change ONE per test): land Loot ±1, Casterly/Oldtown Defense 6/5 → 5/4 if never sacked; Wrath 1 → 2 crew if leader wins by 4+; Bounty +1 → +2 if kingmaking.
- Target win-rate after 6 plays: each faction 25–40%, no faction 0% or 60%+.

## Variants (ship in MVP4)

- Learning game: 3 Seasons, ignore Storm + 4/6 miracles ( = MVP2 map with MVP3 cards).
- 2-player: remove Orkmont + Flint's Finger, first to 10 Legend wins.
