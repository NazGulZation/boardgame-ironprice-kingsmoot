# MVP1 — Blood and Salt (playable in 30 min)

**Goal:** prove the core fun — sail out, roll dice, sack a keep — with the smallest possible game.
**Uses:** Dice Rolling (simplified) + tasted Variable Powers + tasted Hand Management.

## What is IN / OUT

- IN: Sail, Muster, Reave. 3 card types. 1 simplified leader power each. Symmetrical ships.
- OUT: Favor track, Storm Belt, Faction cards, Burned/Pillage/Repair, Old Wyk/Orkmont bonuses, Bounty/Wrath, Final Kingsmoot bonuses.

## Components (proxy from current print)

- Map: use only 12 nodes — Pyke, Harlaw, Great Wyk, Ironman's Bay, Sunset N/C/S, and 6 lands: Deepwood Motte (D3 / 3+1), Seagard (D3 / 3+1), Shield Isles (D2 / 2+1), Lannisport (D4 / 4+1), Fair Isle (D2 / 2+1), Arbor (D2 / 2+1). Ignore everything else on `map.png`.
- Dice: 3 normal d6. Convert: 6=Kraken (2 hits), 4–5=Axe (1 hit), 3=Shield (block), 1–2=miss.
- Cards: 12 cards only — 4x Boarding Party (+2 dice attacking), 4x Shield Wall (block 2), 4x Full Sail (Sail 3, +2 move). From `cards-print.pdf` pages 1–2, or proxy with index cards.
- Pieces: 2 ships per player (ignore 3rd), 8 crew each, Hoard + Legend tokens.

## Factions (minimal powers — the only asymmetry)

- **Euron:** once per Season, re-roll any of your dice.
- **Victarion:** +1 die when attacking (max 5 dice total in MVP1).
- **Asha:** after winning a raid with no losses, draw 1 card. May retreat free after round 1.
- Ships: all Move 2, Carry 4. Hand size 4 for all. Start: 5 Hoard, 8 crew (4 aboard, 4 ashore at home port: Euron=Pyke, Asha=Harlaw, Victarion=Great Wyk).

## Setup (3 min)

1. Place ships + 4 crew at home ports. Shuffle 12 cards, deal 4 each (Euron/Victarion/Asha all 4 in MVP1).
2. Youngest player first. Play **3 Seasons**. Each Season = each player takes **2 Turns**. Each Turn = **2 Actions**: Sail (move 1 fleet up to 2), Muster (spend 3 Hoard → +3 crew at home), Reave (see below).

## Reave / Battle (max 2 rounds)

- Must be adjacent to target. Attacker dice = ceil(crew/2) + card bonus (max 5). Defender (land) = Defense value in dice, rolled once per round. Rival fleet = ceil(crew/2).
- Round: both roll, each may play **1 card**. Kraken=2 hits, Axe=1, Shield=blocks 1. Remove 1 crew per unblocked hit (defender first, then attacker takes hits).
- Win = defender wiped OR beats land Defense hits. Take Hoard+Legend immediately. Land is **looted out** (cannot be raided again this Season — mark with coin). No Burned in MVP1.
- If all your ships empty: respawn at home with 1 ship + 3 crew free.

## Win

- **First to 5 Legend wins immediately**, else most Legend after 3 Seasons. Tie → most Hoard → most crew ashore.

## Playtest questions (advance only if YES x2 plays)

1. Did every player sack at least 2 keeps in 30 min? (If no → lower Defenses to 2/2/3.)
2. Did dice feel decisive but not random-broken? (If Victarion wins both → drop his bonus to first round only.)
3. Did anyone hoard cards without playing? (If yes → hand management needs MVP2.)
