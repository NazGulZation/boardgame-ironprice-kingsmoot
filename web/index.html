📜 Rules Quick Guide — IRON PRICE: Kingsmoot

1. Objective

Amass the most Legend after 5 Seasons to be crowned King of Salt and Rock on Old Wyk.

2. Turn Structure

Each season has 3 turns per player. On your turn, take 2 Actions:

  • Sail: Move a fleet up to 2 nodes along sea routes.
  • Muster: At a controlled port, pay 3 Hoard (2 at Great Wyk) for +3 crew.
  • Reave: Attack an adjacent Green Land keep with your fleet.
  • Pray: Discard an action for +1 Drowned Favor.

3. Reaving Green Lands

Roll 1 die per 2 crew (max 6). Keep rolls Defense dice. Kraken = 2 hits, Axe = 1 hit, Shield = 1 block. Beat Defense to claim Hoard & Legend! Sacked keeps become Burned.

4. Season End Scoring

Gain +1 Legend per Iron Isle occupied. Refreshes 2 burned keeps each season.
